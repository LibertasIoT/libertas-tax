# Smith family sample papers

**Synthetic test data. Never file these documents.** Every page is marked
"SYNTHETIC TEST DATA — NOT A TAX DOCUMENT". Names, addresses, EINs and
accounts are fictional.

These are the 65 tax papers that the latest automatic QA walk imported into
the
[U.S. tax return App](https://github.com/LibertasIoT/libertas-doc/blob/main/examples/federal-tax.md).
Try them yourself: in the App's Document center, choose **Import with AI** and
select one batch at a time: it reads up to eight papers at once.

**[Download all 65 papers (zip, 3.4 MB)](smith-family-2026.zip)**

For a simple single return, try [Casey Sample](../single-w2-2026/) first.

## The family

John and Jane Smith file jointly. John is 65 and blind; he works until
retiring in June and receives retirement benefits. Jane is 41 and starts
consulting after early unemployment. Alice, 19, is their college student;
Bob, 7, is their child with a disability, cared for while both parents work.
The family also has investments, a small farm, a commercial rental and
partnership interests.

The interview then asks what no paper says, such as last year's return; answer
as you like.
